# Family account

A **family account** brings together the net worth and the budget of a couple, each keeping **their own account**:
each person enters their data on their side, exports it every month, and the family account imports it. The
Net worth page (with its history), the Budget page and the report then show **the family**, or **each person**.

![Family members](../captures/famille-ordinateur.png)

## Setting up

1. Each person creates their Patrimeter account and enters their accounts, their balances and, if they wish, their
   [budget](budget.md).
2. Create a **third account**, for the family (another email address).
3. In this account, open **Family members** (link of the welcome Net worth page, or of the [Your data](donnees.md) page),
   then **Add a member**: their name and their export file.

A family account brings together **two members at most**.

## What is shared: each person's share

The family **adds up** its members' data: so that nothing is counted twice, each person enters **their share** of
what is shared.

| Shared | What each person enters |
|---|---|
| Joint account | half of its balance (or their share) |
| House bought together | the property with its **share** (50%…) and its linked loan, pro rata (see [Real estate](biens.md)) |
| Rent, groceries, bills | their share of each expense, in their budget |

The family account can also have **its own accounts**, the children's for example: they count in the family.

## Every month

1. Each person downloads their data: **Your data** → **Download my data**.
2. In the family account, **Family members** → **Update** for each member, with their new file: it **replaces** the
   previous import.

For each member, the page shows the date of the last import and the date of the file; a file from a previous month
is flagged (**Warning**). **Removing** a member deletes their imported data from the family account, never from
their own account.

## Family views

As soon as a member is imported, the Net worth page, the report to share and the budget offer a choice at
the top of the page:

- **Whole family** (by default): the members' accounts and the family account's own; the budget brings together the
  members' budgets;
- **one member**: their data only, as they see it in their account;
- **This account**: the family account's own data, if it has any.

The choice is kept from one page to the next. In the family:

- imported accounts carry the name of their member ("Savings account · Alex") and cannot be changed;
- the **budget** is read-only: each member becomes an adult, with their income and all their categories; there is
  no joint account to split, since each person entered their share. Children with the same first name are brought
  together, and their cost adds up both budgets; the emergency savings target is the higher of the two;
- the Net worth page [recommendations](tableau-de-bord.md#recommendations) compare the family's emergency savings with
  the family's expenses.

## Privacy

- Imported data is **encrypted with the family account's key**, like all wealth data: the server administrator
  cannot read it.
- The export file is not encrypted: delete it once imported.
- The family account's export only contains its **own** data, not its members'.
