# Indicators

An **indicator** is a total you define yourself: pyramid levels and chosen accounts, followed every month as the net
worth. For instance, "Retirement": the Shares level and a life insurance; or "Available savings": the emergency
savings and a securities account.

## Creating an indicator

On the **Net worth** page, **Indicators**, then **New indicator**:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name | 60 characters at most |
| Pyramid levels | The net total of each ticked level (assets minus loans) |
| Accounts | The ticked accounts; an account that already belongs to a ticked level is counted once |
| Also show its share of the net worth | The percentage of the net worth that the indicator represents |

At least one level or one account is needed; 10 indicators at most.

## Following your indicators

- The **Indicators** page gives the value of each one this month and its change over a month.
- The **Net worth** page shows them in the **My indicators** box, for the month displayed.
- The name of an indicator opens its page: its **last twelve months**, the change and the deletion.

The values are worked out as the net worth: ownership shares and linked loans included, a month without a balance
carrying over the previous one. Like your other data, the indicators are encrypted and are part of the
[export](donnees.md).
