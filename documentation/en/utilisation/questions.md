# Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to enter every account every month?**
No: an account without a balance keeps its last known balance (carried over). Enter what changes.

**How do I enter a loan?**
Like an account whose nature is liability, with the outstanding principal as a positive amount: Patrimeter
subtracts it.

**I forgot my password.**
"Forgotten your password?" link; with your recovery key, you keep your data
([security](securite.md#forgotten-password)).

**I lost my phone.**
Sign in with a backup code, then change app in My account.

**Can the administrator see my amounts?**
No: they are encrypted with a key that only your password (or your recovery key) opens. The administrator can,
however, see your address, the number of your accounts and the months entered, and the sign-in log
([application security](../SECURITY.md)).

**Can I change server?**
Yes: [export your data](donnees.md), then import it on the new instance.

**Can the site be used without JavaScript?**
Yes: every screen works without JavaScript, which only adds comfort (chart, month calendars, type lists you can
type into).

**Which version of Patrimeter is installed?**
It is shown at the bottom of every page, sign-in page included ("Patrimeter, version X.Y.Z").

**I started Patrimeter in November: how do I enter the months before?**
Go to the month you want, then **Copy balances from another month** from November: the accounts opened since
are included, and saving moves their opening date back
([monthly update](saisie.md#copying-balances-from-another-month)).

**After an update, the screen has not changed.**
Reload the page, bypassing the browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R). If nothing changes, the administrator checks
the server's update ([checking the update](../exploitation/mise-a-jour.md#2-on-linux)).

**How do I switch to French?**
Click the language button (globe and **EN**), next to the theme button, then **Français**; same way to come
back to **English**. The choice is remembered on the device.

**Why is the "Monthly update" menu in bold, with a red dot?**
No balance of the current month is entered yet: it reminds you, and goes back to normal as soon as you enter it. An
email reminder is also possible ([My account, monthly reminder](securite.md#monthly-reminder)).

**Where are my savings goals?**
**Goals** menu to follow them; **Monthly update**, **Goals** tab, to create and change them
([savings goals](objectifs.md)).

**Can I try Patrimeter without creating an account?**
Yes, if the instance offers the demo: **Try the demo** on the sign-in page. The account and its fictitious data are
deleted after 2 hours or at sign-out ([trying without an account](premiere-connexion.md#trying-without-an-account)).
